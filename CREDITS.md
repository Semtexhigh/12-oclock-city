Downloaded but **not used in the build** (kept in `_tmp/unused_models`): Electric off-road dirt bike (Macai140) and BMX Bike (Erdem Dağdelen). Neither has separate wheel or fork parts, so they can't animate. The procedural bikes keep spinning wheels and steering. Also not used: the Range Rover Sport (900k triangles; wouldn't simplify), McDonald's restaurant (1.1M triangles), the Scud missile (too heavy) and three boats that were rejected on quality.
